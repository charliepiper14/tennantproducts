Activated Bamboo Charcoal Cold Launch

Tennant Products · Meta Ads · Prepared by Charles Piper, Heath Media

Overview

This document contains the full build specification for the Activated Bamboo Charcoal cold acquisition campaign on Meta. Every creative, headline, description, and destination URL has been prepared for direct build in Ads Manager.

The structure is one campaign (CBO) with two ad sets and eight ads. Ad set one targets the health-conscious detox audience and routes to /pages/aw-yjrfsw. Ad set two targets the morning-after angle for women 40 to 55, routed to /pages/aw-lpgdwb. Daily budget is $100 at the campaign level, distributed dynamically between the two ad sets by Meta. Both ad sets optimise for Purchase from day one, given the account pixel has sufficient Purchase history from the DE and BF+ campaigns. The offer is 20% off plus free shipping, one-time purchase only, with the 10% pop-up suppressed on both landing pages.

Every destination URL carries the openExternalBrowser=1 parameter so the Meta in-app browser hands off correctly. Creative samples for each ad are shown alongside the spec, with videos playable inline. Backup creative is listed in the reserve section for week two refresh.

Creative excluded from this launch

The video file _Video__Activated_Bamboo_Charcoal_2_.mp4 contains on-screen health claim language referencing heavy metals, pathogenic microbes, and electric charge mechanisms. This framing carries a material risk of Meta ad rejection and FTC structure-function exposure. The video has been excluded from the launch set. If the client wishes to use it, the text layer needs rewording first.

Pre-launch checklist

  • Test order placed through each LP with discount applied, tracked end to end across Meta, PMA, and GA4

Ads Manager · Charles

  • Campaign built with the name and settings from section 1
  • Both ad sets built with the names and settings from section 1
  • All 8 ads built with copy, headline, description, and URL pasted from sections 2 and 3
  • Every URL carries openExternalBrowser=1 and the full UTM string
  • Advantage+ Creative enhancements DISABLED on every ad
  • All ads in Active status (not Limited or Rejected) after Meta review
  • Page and Instagram identity linked on every ad
  • Preview checked across Feed, Reels, and Stories placements
  • Launch time set to Thursday 8pm US CT for Friday morning data start
06

Post-launch monitoring

First 24 hours

  • LPV rate should hold above 70%. Below that, check the openExternalBrowser parameter and Cloudflare rules.
  • CTR floor is 1.0%. A low reading on day two is a flag to note, not an action point.
  • Any ads showing Limited or Rejected status should be re-appealed immediately.

Days 2 to 7 — no-touch window

Budgets, audiences, copy, URLs, and creative remain untouched for seven full days. The only reasons to break this window are a tracking failure, Meta rejection, landing page outage, or Cloudflare challenging paid traffic.

Day 8 onwards

  • Review Purchase cost per ad set. If blended ROAS is above 2.0x, propose a campaign budget lift to $150/day.
  • Underperforming ads paused, top performers continued.
  • Creative refresh brief goes to the design team for two new statics per ad set to test against the initial set.
  • Full week-one reporting delivered to Janice in the standard weekly deck format.
