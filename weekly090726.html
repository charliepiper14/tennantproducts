The one line that matters: Google Ads is delivering a 10.1x return on the client week just closed, and it has now held between 10.1x and 10.4x for three straight weeks. That is more than double the 4 to 5x target set on the tracking sheet. The engine is proven, the measurement is clean, and the account is running on roughly $69 a day of actual spend against the $340 a day budget the business has allocated to Google. Revenue dipped 21.8 percent this week only because spend was pulled back 19.5 percent, not because efficiency softened. The Performance Max campaign is the clearest signal in the account: it spent right at its $35 a day ceiling while returning 9.1x, which means it is turning away demand it could profitably serve. This report reads the week honestly, then lays out the staged plan to scale a channel that is currently the strongest performing and most under invested lever the business has.

Two ceilings are throttling the channel, not the market. The first is that the campaigns themselves are only set to spend $82.50 a day combined, so even if demand were infinite the account would stop at a fraction of the allocated budget. The second is that Performance Max is spending $35.55 a day against a $35 daily cap, which is Google telling us plainly that it has more profitable demand to serve and no room to serve it. When a 9x campaign is pinned to its budget, the correct move is to feed it, in controlled steps that protect the bidding model.
What scaling responsibly looks like here. The path is not a single jump to $340 a day. Why This Is The Number One Scale Move
Performance Max spent $35.55 a day against a $35.00 daily cap and still returned 9.11x. A campaign that fully consumes its budget and returns nine times the spend is, by definition, leaving profitable demand on the table. Its impressions rose 67.6 percent week on week to 19,389, which shows the cross network surface has appetite to serve far more than the current budget allows. The 50.7 percent drop in conversion value looks alarming in isolation, but it is measured against a prior week that ran at an exceptional 21x, a level no campaign holds indefinitely. The honest read is that 9x is the durable performance and 21x was a favourable spike. A durable 9x pinned to its budget is the single clearest instruction the account is giving us: raise this budget.
How to scale it without breaking the bidding model. Performance Max runs on a Smart Bidding model that relearns whenever budget moves sharply, so the discipline is measured steps rather than one large jump. The plan is to raise the daily budget by roughly 20 to 30 percent every three to four days while ROAS holds above target, moving $35 toward $50, then $65, then $85 a day across the coming fortnight. Each step is held long enough to confirm the return before the next. This lets the model expand its winning audience and placement mix on stable footing rather than resetting learning with a shock.
The feed and structure are ready to absorb the spend. The campaign is serving cleanly across the cross network surface at a $1.57 CPC, the lowest cost traffic anywhere in the account, and the Merchant Center feed is live with no product disapprovals blocking delivery. Once budget headroom is opened, the next structural lever is to widen coverage with additional asset groups built around the proven sellers, Restore, Digestive Enzymes and the GI 60 line, so the extra budget flows into distinct product demand rather than concentrating on a single hero set. That keeps efficiency intact as volume climbs.

The account is showing for iodine searches and capturing none of them. Across the client week, iodine related terms generated 103 impressions at the word level with zero clicks and zero spend, because the dedicated Iodine Search campaign is enabled but not actually serving or bidding. Lugol's Iodine is a named best seller and the searches are real, high intent and non brand, which is precisely the new to brand demand the marketing goal calls for. This is not a hypothetical audience to test into, it is proven demand already appearing in the auction and going to competitors. jcrowsllc.com, a direct iodine competitor, is holding 35 percent impression share on this shared auction. Activating the Iodine campaign against a proper Lugol's landing page is the cleanest net new revenue lever in the account.
Two structural gaps compound the miss. The phrase match keywords added to Branded, and the iodine phrase terms, are all sitting at zero cost and zero clicks, which means they are live in the account but not yet triggering, either because bids are too low to enter the auction or the campaign is budget starved. Dynamic Search, the campaign whose whole job is to catch non brand long tail queries the keyword lists miss, is funded at just $7.50 a day. The account has three separate mechanisms for capturing non brand demand, iodine search, phrase match and DSA, and all three are effectively switched off or starved. Turning them on in sequence is where scale beyond brand comes from.
Small brand adjacent leakage to tidy at the same time. The search terms show minor spend bleeding to non customer queries: tennantinstitute at $6.88, the dr tenant misspelling at $2.84, and tennant institute at $0.10. None is large, but at exact match negative level they are a five minute fix that stops the DSA and broad matching from paying for the wrong intent as budgets rise. Worth doing now so the leaks do not scale with the spend.

The Scale Plan Phased And Protected

Next 6 weeks
Growth Roadmap
Phase 1, Weeks 1 to 2
Unlock the proven engine and tidy the leaks.

Raise Performance Max from $35 toward $50 then $65 a day, in 20 to 30 percent steps every three to four days while ROAS holds above target. Clear the disapproved Branded ads and apply the pending recommendations to lift the optimisation score from 64.8 percent into the mid eighties. Add exact match negatives for tennantinstitute and the dr tenant misspelling. Hold Branded budget at $40, it is demand capped.
Phase 2, Weeks 2 to 3
Switch on the dormant demand pools.

Activate the Iodine Search campaign properly, correct bids and budget so it enters the auction, pointed at a dedicated Lugol's landing page. The 103 iodine impressions already appearing prove the demand is live. Raise Dynamic Search from $7.50 to $15 to $20 a day so it gathers stable signal on non brand long tail. Confirm the phrase match keywords are bidding into the auction rather than sitting idle.
Phase 3, Weeks 3 to 6
Scale toward the allocated budget.

Continue stepping Performance Max toward $85 to $100 a day. Add asset groups for Restore, Digestive Enzymes and GI 60 so new budget flows into distinct product demand. Once conversion volume supports it, introduce a target ROAS on Performance Max set deliberately toward the 5 to 6x band to trade a slice of efficiency for materially more volume, moving the account toward the $340 a day capacity at the client's own 4 to 5x target.
The guardrails that keep scale safe. Move budgets in steps of roughly 20 to 30 percent, never a single large jump, and hold each step three to four days before the next so Smart Bidding does not reset its learning. Change one major lever per campaign at a time. Keep the standardised UTM tracking templates in place across every campaign so attribution stays clean as spend rises, the work already scoped on Branded, DSA and the Performance Max feed. Cross reference Google conversions against Shopify order data weekly so we scale on verified revenue, not just platform reported value.
Sequencing matters more than speed. The order is deliberate: fix and unlock what is already proven before switching on anything new, because clean campaigns scale predictably and messy ones amplify their own problems. Performance Max first because it is pinned and proven. Branded tidied next because the fixes are free efficiency. Iodine and DSA third because they are net new demand that needs the tracking and negatives clean before they spend. By week three the account should be spending roughly double the current daily rate while holding comfortably above the target return, with a clear line of sight to the full allocated budget.
