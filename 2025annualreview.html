Key Insights & Learnings

What Worked in 2025

  • Total Shopify revenue of $4.57M with 10,628 orders
  • Ad spend efficiency at just 1.8% of revenue
  • CAC:LTV ratio never dropped below 1:12 (4x industry)
  • 80.6% returning customer rate (Q4)
  • Meta retargeting consistently strong (7.35x ROAS)
  • Branded search maintained 99%+ impression share
  • DSA campaign efficiency at $16 CPA

Areas Requiring Attention

  • ROAS declined 89% overall (Q1→Q4)
  • AOV dropped 53% from Q1 ($510) to Q4 ($240)
  • CPA peaked at $74+ in Q3
  • Google impression share critically low (10-12%)
  • Q1 metrics inflated by view-through attribution
  • Expiring inventory (Probiotic Feb, Brain Food+ Apr)
  • Female audience share declining (-9.6pp)

Critical Understanding

The Q1 2025 ROAS figures were inflated by view-through attribution. Q4 metrics (3.5-4x ROAS) represent realistic direct conversion performance. This explains the apparent "decline" - it's largely attribution normalization rather than business deterioration. The underlying unit economics remained exceptional with CAC:LTV never below 1:12.
