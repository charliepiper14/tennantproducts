Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What is Lugol's Iodine and how is it different from regular supplements?
Lugol's Iodine is a specific solution containing two forms of iodine: molecular iodine (I₂) and potassium iodide (KI). This dual-form approach was developed by French physician Jean Lugol in the 1800s and remains the clinical standard in integrative medicine. Standard supplements typically contain only one form, limiting which tissues can absorb and utilize it. Dr. Tennant's formula provides both at the 5% clinical concentration, ensuring whole-body support rather than thyroid-only.
How much should I take and how quickly will I see results?
Start with 1 drop per day in a full glass of water and gradually increase to 2–3 drops as tolerated. Most customers report noticeable energy and mental clarity within 2–4 weeks. Thyroid normalization and hormonal benefits are typically seen at the 60–90 day mark. Individual results vary based on your level of deficiency.
Can I take Lugol's Iodine if I'm on thyroid medication?
If you are on thyroid medication (Synthroid, levothyroxine, Armour Thyroid, etc.), consult your physician before starting iodine supplementation. Increasing iodine intake can affect thyroid hormone production and may require a dosage adjustment. Many integrative physicians actively support iodine supplementation alongside medication, but this should be monitored through bloodwork.
What cofactors should I take with iodine for best results?
Dr. Tennant recommends: Selenium (200mcg daily) to protect the thyroid during iodine loading; Vitamin C (500mg+) to support detoxification; Magnesium for cellular uptake; and Vitamins B2 and B3 to support the iodine transport system. Tennant Products' Iodine Cofactors formula provides all of these in the appropriate clinical ratios.
