Holiday Period Context

Understanding the Seasonal Pattern

What We Are Seeing:

The week of December 17-23 is historically one of the lowest-converting periods for health supplement purchases. Consumer attention shifts to holiday shopping, gift-giving, and year-end activities. This is a normal pattern that typically resolves in the first week of January when "new year, new health" motivations kick in.
