No, it was not a mistake - and the data confirms the campaign was genuinely working. 4.11x blended ROAS, $73 CPP, 144 purchases, and a landing page converting at 65.6% LPV-to-ATC. These are strong results for a premium supplement. The decision to wind down was driven by inventory constraints and a successful stock clearance objective, not poor performance. Now that Probiotics GI 60 is in stock, there is a compelling case to relaunch. We have proven creatives, a validated audience, and accumulated pixel data to build from. This will not start from zero.
Question 2: Why is Google ROAS at 0.6x and Meta at 0.9x?
Google 0.58x: The root cause is PMax generating $0 in Revenue on $418 spend. PMax is losing over 90% of auctions due to ad rank, caused by a combination of an overly aggressive Target ROAS bid setting and an active policy limitation ("Eligible (Limited)") that restricts which inventory is available to the campaign. Competitor bidding from southeasternequipment.net and tennanthealth.com is a contributing but not primary factor. Branded Search recovered well this week ($388 revenue), confirming the prior week's zero was an attribution timing issue. The fix for Google requires escalating the PMax policy issue to Google Support and reviewing the bid strategy target.
Meta 0.83x: The DE campaign is the drag. The DE CBO spent $1,043.55 with 1 purchase and $493.81 in conv value (0.47x ROAS) as the campaign accumulates ATC signals toward the 50-event threshold needed for Purchase optimisation. This is planned and expected. Excluding DE, the BF+ CBO returned 2.02x ROAS with 5 purchases on $313.37. Once DE transitions to Purchase optimisation (estimated April 17-20), Meta ROAS should recover materially. The week's positive news on Meta is that the Shopify metrics were strong: $50,772 gross sales, 44 new customers, and checkout completion recovering to 55.6%.
Cross-referencing your PMA sheet (column AR, 2026 tab): The figures you referenced match our platform data. I always cross-reference our platform exports against the PMA sheet during performance analysis, and the numbers are consistent. The 0.6x Google and 0.9x Meta ROAS readings you pulled are accurate for the period in question. The underlying picture is more nuanced than the top-line ROAS suggests: Shopify revenue was up 23.6%, new customers grew 46.7%, and CAC is improving. The ROAS suppression is concentrated in two specific structural issues (PMax policy limit and DE learning phase) that are both addressable.
