Strategic Action Plan

Priority Actions for Performance Recovery

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (This Week)

1. Reinstate Meta Retargeting Campaign

Why: Previous period delivered 4.67x ROAS at $53.40 CPA. This is your highest-efficiency traffic source and it is currently offline.

Action Steps:

  • Reactivate "SS - Retargeting" campaign
  • Set daily budget to $50-100/day to start
  • Segment by recency: 7-day, 14-day, 30-day website visitors
  • Use the proven Brand Video creative that was working

Expected Impact: 10-15 additional purchases per month at under $60 CPA

2. Diagnose and Fix DSA Campaign Collapse

Why: Conversions dropped 83.54% from 9.65 to 1.59. This needs immediate investigation.

Investigation Steps:

  • Review search terms report for irrelevant queries
  • Check for any landing page changes or errors
  • Review competitor activity in the iodine and supplement space
  • Verify conversion tracking is firing correctly

Potential Fixes:

  • Add negative keywords to filter low-quality traffic
  • Increase budget from $15/day to $30/day for more learning volume
  • Consider switching to manual bidding temporarily

3. Evaluate Omnipresent Campaign

Current State: $1,356.57 spend for 9 purchases = $150.73 CPA, 1.09x ROAS. Nearly breakeven.

Decision Framework:

  • If target is efficiency: Pause and reallocate budget to Probiotic and Retargeting
  • If target is awareness: Accept lower ROAS but set a CPA cap at $125 (breakeven)
  • Review individual ad sets for any hidden winners that could be scaled separately

Recommendation: Reduce budget to $25/day maximum and funnel remaining budget to Retargeting once reactivated.

SHORT-TERM ACTIONS (Weeks 2-3)

4. Scale Probiotic Campaign Winners

Current Performance: 35 purchases at $68.75 CPA with 3.33x ROAS. Below your $75 target CPA.

Scale Strategy:

  • Increase daily budget from $80/day to $120/day (50% increase)
  • Duplicate "Social Post 7 - Video" into new ad sets for testing
  • Create 3-5 new testimonial variations using the same format
  • Test new audiences: gut health interest stacks, competitor followers

5. Launch Traditional Search Campaign

Why: Currently relying 100% on automation (PMax + DSA). Need exact/phrase match control.

Recommended Structure:

  • Ad Group 1: Branded terms (tennant products, dr tennant, tennant supplements)
  • Ad Group 2: Iodine terms (lugol's iodine, iodine supplement, thyroid iodine)
  • Ad Group 3: Probiotic terms (best probiotic, gut health probiotic)
  • Ad Group 4: Restore terms (dr tennant restore, cellular nutrition)

Budget: Start at $50/day with Target ROAS 3.0x

6. Address Impression Share Crisis

Current State: Under 10% impression share. Amazon at 54%.

Strategy Options (Require Approval):

  • Increase PMax budget from $75/day to $100-125/day
  • Lower Target ROAS from current level to 2.5x temporarily
  • Fix PMax policy violations (asset groups showing "limited by policy")
  • Add more asset variations to improve ad strength

Realistic Target: Improve from under 10% to 20-25% impression share within 30 days

MEDIUM-TERM ACTIONS (Weeks 4-8)

7. Creative Refresh Program

  • Produce 5-8 new UGC testimonial videos per month
  • Create product-specific angle variations (Iodine story, Restore story, Probiotic story)
  • Test Dr. Tennant authority content featuring his credentials and healing journey
  • Develop comparison content against competitors (AG1, Seed, etc.)

8. Audience Development

  • Build 1% Lookalike from top 25% revenue customers
  • Create 1% Lookalike from repeat purchasers
  • Test interest stacks: holistic medicine, functional medicine, biohacking
  • Implement proper exclusions: recent purchasers (30 days), high-frequency viewers
