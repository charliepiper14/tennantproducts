Headline: Strong January Recovery

January delivered a solid rebound from December's holiday-adjusted lull. Shopify gross sales surged 25.9% to $264,210, driven by higher order volume (+17.5%) and a rising AOV ($300). The returning customer base grew nearly 20%, reflecting strong retention. Meta Ads ROAS climbed to 4.47x, the highest account-level efficiency we've seen, while Google Ads scaled spend 110% with PMax maintaining a healthy 4.37x ROAS. The main watchpoint is Google Branded search costs, which spiked 344% and require immediate investigation.

Year-Over-Year Context

While MoM trends are strongly positive, gross sales are down 37.9% compared to January 2025 ($264K vs $426K). This gap largely reflects the discontinuation of high-ticket BioTransducer device sales and several product SKU retirements (Chocolate Restore, Brain Food packets, Percepta). Supplement-only revenue is on a healthier trajectory when these product line changes are factored out.

Critical: Branded Search Spend Spike: $1,700 (↑344% MoM)

The SS - Branded campaign jumped from $383 in December to $1,700 in January, a 344% increase. Average CPC jumped from $2.13 to $6.32 (+197%). Despite generating 13 conversions (vs 12 in Dec), the ROAS collapsed from 9.53x to 1.52x. This is the primary driver of overall Google ROAS deterioration.

Root cause investigation:Three new branded keywords were added in January: "tennant institute" ($1,079 spend alone, 77 clicks), "dr tennant products" ($241, 55 clicks), and "dr tennant supplements" ($107, 19 clicks). These keywords ate budget aggressively without converting at the same rate as the original "tennant products" exact match. The "tennant institute" keyword in particular is likely capturing informational/clinic-seeking traffic, not purchase-intent traffic.

Recommended action:Pause "tennant institute" keyword immediately (or move to negative list if not relevant to product sales). Review CPC bids on the new branded keywords to control costs. The original "tennant products" keyword performed efficiently in December at $383/$2.13 CPC. Restore that as the primary branded anchor.

9February 2026 Strategic Recommendations

Prioritized actions for the coming month

Immediate Actions (This Week)

1. Google Branded Search Cleanup

Add "institute", "integrative medicine", and "texas" as negative keywords in the SS - Branded campaign. Consider pausing the "tennant institute" exact match keyword entirely. This one change should recover $900/month in wasted spend and restore Branded ROAS closer to the 9.53x level seen in December. Monitor the "dr tennant products" and "dr tennant supplements" keywords. They're worth keeping but need CPC bid caps ($3-4 max) to prevent CPC inflation.

2. Brain Food+ CBO Campaign Restructure

The Brain Food campaign is spending $907/month at 1.71x ROAS with most spend going to underperforming video and image ads. Pause all ads with zero purchases (Video Ad 1, Image Ad 1, Image Ad 3, Video Ad 2). Reallocate budget toward proven winners like the "Don't Trust Labels" creative. Consider combining the Adv+ and RTG ad sets into a single Advantage+ Shopping campaign for better optimization signal.

High Priority (Next 1-2 Weeks)

3. Meta Retargeting Creative Refresh

With a frequency of 18.9, creative fatigue is imminent. Produce 2-3 new retargeting creatives that build on the winning formats: (1) A new comparison-style static ad, (2) A fresh UGC testimonial video (Social Post 7 format), and (3) A new value-proposition angle for the Restore line. Also expand the retargeting audience window from 30-day to 60-day website visitors to lower frequency and increase reach.

4. Google PMax Asset Group Policy Review

Some PMax asset groups are flagged as "limited by policy." Audit all asset groups, resolve any policy violations (likely image or text compliance issues), and re-submit. Unlocking these could expand impression share beyond the current 14.7% and drive additional conversion volume.

5. Probiotic Transition Strategy

The legacy Probiotic Formula dropped -57% while the new GI 60 Probiotic launched at $6,800 in its first full month. Total probiotic category revenue is $9,888 (Jan) vs $7,240 (Dec for legacy alone). The transition is net positive. Develop dedicated ad creative for GI 60 to accelerate the transition. Consider a bundle offer (GI 60 + Digestive Enzymes) to cross-sell and recover the Digestive Enzyme decline (-17%).

Growth Opportunities (February Planning)

6. Scale Google PMax

PMax scaled 64% in January while maintaining 4.37x ROAS. Test a further 20-25% budget increase in February (from $55/day to $68/day). With the Branded cleanup saving $900/month, this can be budget-neutral at the account level while shifting spend to the higher-performing campaign.

7. Relaunch Prospecting on Meta

December's SS - Prospecting campaign delivered 4.36x ROAS on the "Probiotic All" ad set. With the GI 60 now in market and January's strong store performance, February is a good time to relaunch a prospecting campaign, potentially targeting the probiotic/gut health angle with fresh creative. Start at $15-20/day and scale based on 7-day ROAS signals.

8. Google DSA Scale Test

DSA delivered 5.08x ROAS at only $227/month, the most efficient campaign in the Google account. Test increasing budget from $7.50/day to $15/day. Monitor impression share and conversion rate at the higher spend level.

9. Discount Strategy Review

January's discount rate jumped to 14.7% ($38,823 in discounts on $264K gross). While discounts drive conversion, this is approaching a level that could compress margins. Review which discount codes are being used, whether automatic discounts are stacking, and whether retargeting could drive conversions at a lower discount threshold (e.g., 10% instead of 20%).
