Even with the acknowledged DSA outlier in the ROAS calculation, the CAC figure is independent of that outlier (it is spend divided by new customers). This tells a consistent story: the account is acquiring new customers efficiently at current spend levels. The lever to grow now is absolute volume of new customers, which means unblocking the DE campaign delivery and launching GI 60.
