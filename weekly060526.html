The catalog override was the dominant cause of suppressed performance, confirmed in detail by Gregory Silvano (Buyist) on Apr 28. Meta's Advantage+ Catalog Ads behaviour was silently rewriting the ad destination URL with the canonical Shopify PDP variant URL (variant=9584282501167) for the tagged video creative. Three confirmation signals: openExternalBrowser=1 was stripped from the captured URL even though it was typed into the ad URL, indicating Meta replaced rather than appended; variant=9584282501167 is a Meta catalog feed parameter that Auware never generates; the captured URL contained Meta's full Dynamic URL Parameter set (campaign_id, ad_id, utm_id, utm_term) which is added when Advantage+ Catalog features are active. The Cloudflare logs filtered to Meta-referrer traffic showed zero hits to /pages/aw-yjrfsw because Meta was sending those clicks straight to /products/activated-bamboo-charcoal. The fix is to disconnect the Shopify Facebook and Instagram sales channel auto-sync that was repushing the canonical PDP URL into the Meta catalog item, then disable Advantage+ Destination on the campaign. Implemented Mon May 4 outside this reporting window.
The signals beneath the override are positive. CTR at 1.36% on cold traffic, CPC at $0.94, and 1,121 link clicks across the week tell us the ad creative is working as designed at the top of funnel. The 11 adds to cart and 13 checkouts initiated against only 74 measured LPVs means the small subset of clicks that escaped the catalog rewrite (probably desktop placements and the narrow window where openExternalBrowser briefly held) demonstrated cart engagement consistent with cold benchmarks. The week-over-week ABC organic Shopify lift held at $1,065.50 in net sales versus $1,010.50 the prior week, confirming the audience-product fit. Once the catalog override fix takes effect, the campaign should deliver into the 50 to 70% LPV band that produces a measurable purchase contribution.

ABC Catalog Override: What Actually Happened
Three signals confirmed Meta was substituting the destination URL at the platform level rather than the issue sitting in our infrastructure. First, openExternalBrowser=1 was stripped from the captured URL on the live ad despite being typed correctly into Ads Manager, indicating Meta replaced rather than appended. Second, variant=9584282501167 appeared in the captured URL which is a Meta catalog feed parameter that Auware never generates. Third, the captured URL contained Meta's full Dynamic URL Parameter set (campaign_id, ad_id, utm_id, utm_term) added when Advantage+ Catalog features are active. Cloudflare logs filtered to Meta-referrer traffic on /pages/aw-yjrfsw showed zero hits because Meta was sending those clicks straight to /products/activated-bamboo-charcoal at the link-shim level, before any traffic reached our origin. The fix is at the Shopify catalog source: removing Activated Bamboo Charcoal from the Facebook and Instagram sales channel publishing list stops the auto-sync from continuously repushing the canonical PDP URL into the Meta catalog item.
