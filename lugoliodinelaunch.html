Tennant's Winning Angle

Every major competitor sells iodine in isolation. Tennant's is the only Lugol's iodine product that includes the full cofactor stack (Zinc, Selenium, B1, Vitamin C) required for proper iodine metabolism. We are not selling a more expensive version of the same thing. We are selling a fundamentally different, more complete product.

Addressing the Price Gap

Competitors sit at $8-30. Tennant's is $70. The ad messaging must justify the premium by educating on why iodine alone doesn't work. Dr. Tennant's own quote: "You can't just supplement iodine by itself and get the results you need." The cofactors are the story. A 90-day supply at $0.78/day is the value reframe.

The Cross-Sell Upside

Iodine buyers very likely also purchase Restore ($260) and/or Digestive Enzymes ($96). The product page literally directs customers to take iodine with Restore for cofactors.

If the average iodine customer also buys one additional product, the blended AOV could be $150-250+ per customer, not $70.

This completely changes the CPA ceiling. At $200 blended AOV with 40% COGS and 3% fees, breakeven CPA jumps to $114. That means even aggressive prospecting CPAs of $65-80 would be very profitable.

05
Inventory and Supply
Any supply constraints on Iodine Plus capsules or Iodine Liquid? We don't want to scale into out-of-stock (learned from the probiotic situation). How many units on hand? What's the reorder lead time?
06
Cross-Sell Data
Can Janice pull a Shopify report showing what other products Lugol's buyers also purchase? If we know the cross-sell patterns, we can build smarter audience segments and calculate real LTV for iodine-acquired customers. This determines how aggressive we can be on CPA targets.
07
Amazon Channel Conflict
Lugol's Iodine Plus is listed on Amazon. Do we need to account for DTC vs Amazon cannibalization? Should we exclude Amazon shoppers from targeting, or accept that some paid-acquired traffic may convert on Amazon instead of Shopify?
