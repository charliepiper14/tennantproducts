Why This Is The Priority
Performance Max is doing three things at once and doing all of them well. It returned the highest ROAS of any campaign at 17.87x, it grew value fastest at plus 121%, and it did both while never once being allowed to spend beyond $50 per day. The status flag read Eligible (Limited), limited by budget for the full week, which is Google telling us directly that the campaign had qualified demand it could not serve. CPC actually fell within the week as the model found efficiency, and cost per conversion dropped to $15.96. This is a campaign in its strongest phase, being held back by the one lever we fully control.
The Incremental Return Math
Against the prior week, Performance Max took an extra $70.65 of budget and returned an extra $3,122.86 of value. Even discounting that as an unusually strong week, the campaign is clearing 17.87x at the margin. A move from $50 to $65 per day is a 30% step that, even if efficiency halved to 9x on the new spend, would add roughly $135 of value per day, close to $950 per week, at almost no risk. The bidding model already has the signal it needs. The only question is how fast to feed it, which the scale slide answers.
Recommendation, and this is a proposal for Janice to approve, not an action taken. Step Performance Max from $50 to $65 per day as the first move, hold for five to seven days, and read the 7 day rolling ROAS. If it holds above the 4 to 5x floor, which at current performance it will comfortably, step again by a further 20 to 30%. Performance Max rewards budget continuity, so the steps should be deliberate and spaced, never a single large jump that resets the learning phase. The hero feed structure is sound and the optimisation score at 76% has headroom we can lift through the asset and feed actions noted later.

Demand Gen The Cold Acquisition Channel

Setup blueprint and best practice
YouTube, Discover, Gmail
Why Demand Gen, and why now. The account today captures people who already know Dr Tennant, through brand search, and people already shopping the category, through Shopping and DSA. What it does not yet do at any scale is create new demand from strangers. Demand Gen is Google's answer to that, serving video and image creative across YouTube in stream and Shorts, the Discover feed and Gmail, to cold audiences that resemble the account's proven buyers. It is the natural next channel because the account has the two things Demand Gen needs to work, a proven high converting product in iodine, and a full creative set ready to load.
The Five Step Launch, Built On Proven Practice
1

Seed The Audience

Build lookalike and similar segments from the existing customer list and the highest value converters, plus in market segments for supplements, thyroid and gut health. Start warm adjacent, not fully broad, so the model learns fast on the right person.

2

Load The Full Asset Set

Use the sixty asset creative set already briefed, ten hook concepts at 15 and 30 seconds across 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9. Priority five concepts first as vertical and square, since those carry most cold delivery. Full ratios let Google serve every placement.

3

Point At A Proven Page

Drive to the iodine landing page once live, the account's strongest converting product with a dedicated destination, then broaden to Restore and gut health. A cold channel needs the warmest possible page behind it.

4

Bid For Learning First

Open on Maximise Conversions or Maximise Conversion Value with no tROAS constraint so the model can find pockets of demand, then layer a target ROAS once it clears the learning phase and has steady conversions.

5

Give It Runway

Fund a meaningful daily budget and hold it for two to three weeks before judging. Demand Gen needs volume to exit learning, so no changes in the first week, then let spend flow to the winning hooks.

6

Read By Hook

The sixty asset set is built so hooks can be swapped as three second openers on a shared body. Name assets to the convention so reporting maps to concept and hook, and let cost per acquisition on cold decide the winners.

The dependency chain is simple. Ship the iodine landing page, which unlocks Iodine Search and gives Demand Gen its strongest destination. Confirm the priority five creative concepts are cut. Then launch Demand Gen into a proven page with a proper budget and hold your nerve through the learning phase. The creative brief is already written and ready to hand to the video team. This is the channel that takes the account from harvesting existing demand to building new demand, which is what a stated goal of more new to brand customers actually requires.
