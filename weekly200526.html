Attribution Transparency

Three Outliers, Three Explanations
Data Quality Notes
Outlier 1: Brain Copy Retargeting
$3,634.50 conv value from 2 purchases = $1,817.25 AOV per purchase. Brain Copy is the retargeting creative running against website visitors with a Brain Food product focus. The standard Tennant AOV across the account this week is $276.49 blended, $134.43 on the cleanest non-outlier Meta creative (Tess Sprague). A $1,817 AOV is plausible for a Cellular Nutrition Program bundle or a multi-pack Restore Pro order, but not for the Brain Food product the ad creative explicitly promotes. Two scenarios: (1) two returning customers who saw the Brain Copy retargeting ad subsequently bought a large unrelated bundle (cross-product attribution within the 7-day click window), or (2) one or both purchases involve subscription or wholesale-tier pricing. Either way, the $3,634.50 is real attributed value but should not be treated as Brain Copy-driven incremental revenue. The ex-outlier reading is the operational signal.
Outlier 2: Anatomy Hook ABC Attribution Gap
5 ABC purchases attributed at $2,971.98 conv value, against 1 ABC product sold in Shopify at $43 gross / $34.40 net. The Meta-attributed AOV of $594.40 per ABC purchase is mathematically impossible against ABC's $43 retail price. This is the same cross-product attribution dynamic flagged in the prior week's deck but with a wider gap: 5:1 attributed-to-actual on the ABC side. The interpretation: cold ABC ad clicks lead users to the ABC LP, where they then purchase non-ABC products from the broader Tennant catalog (Restore, Iodine, Brain Food, etc.). Meta's 7-day click attribution model credits the full subsequent basket to the ABC click. This dynamic was present throughout April but obscured by the Consentmo LPV suppression; with LPV measurement now intact, the attribution gap is visible and quantifiable. Resolution comes from Janice's duplicated ABC LP test, varying the LP to either successfully convert ABC clicks to ABC purchases (closing the gap), or confirming the LP is structurally a category-entry funnel (in which case the campaign objective and product focus should be reconsidered).
Outlier 3: Monday May 18 Shopify AOV Spike
$11,223.81 gross on 18 orders = $461.94 AOV vs the WTD non-Mon baseline of ~$155. 17 of 18 orders from returning customers (94% return rate vs 79.5% WTD average). 6.7 units per order vs the WTD 2.8 average. Discount load of $2,908.96 (89% of WTD total discount). This is the signature of a Klaviyo bundle/promo send to the existing customer base hitting on Mon May 18. Likely candidate: Restore Pro Vanilla promotion (20 units sold WTD at $4,966 gross, with Mon almost certainly carrying the bulk), or Cellular Nutrition Program bundle (1 sold at $472). Mon's $11K gross is real revenue but should be understood as an email-led activation event rather than a sustainable order-pace pattern. Strip Mon May 18 out, and WTD reads $8,960 across 55 orders at $163 AOV across Sun-Tue-Wed, a 4-day run rate that projects to $15,680 for a 7-day week (vs the prior week's $55,090).
